## Policy

- **core** — recipe lives in `meta-core-rdk`
- **oss-dep** — consumed from poky / OE / OSS layers (no Core recipe)
- **ignored** — out of scope (`InputOutput` / entservices-inputoutput)
- RDK Logger and Westeros are **core** by project decision
